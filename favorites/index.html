Life beyond buttercream

I'm a wife, mom of four, self-taught baker, and homeschool teacher. Between decorating cakes, homeschooling, and keeping up with everyday life, there's always something going on.

My love for baking turned into something I never expected. Over 10 years later, I've created more than 600 custom cakes for birthdays, celebrations and every sweet moment in between.

During the day, I'm teaching the kids, and at night, I'm in the kitchen baking. But wifing and momming never stops! Batter N' Life is my little corner to share it all: the baking, the busy days, and the sweet moments in between.

Thank you for being here, whether you came for the buttercream or the motherhood.

With love, from my kitchen ♥
